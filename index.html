Company Overview

What TCS Does

TCS provides IT services, consulting and business solutions to large global enterprises. Its offering combines consulting, technology services, engineering and industry solutions that help clients modernise their systems and run them efficiently.

IT Services

Dividend History

Dividend per Share: FY22 – FY26

Total dividends over five years were ₹467 per share, an average of ₹93.4 a year. Payments rose from ₹43 in FY22 to ₹126 in FY25, then eased to ₹110 in FY26. Special dividends (₹67 in FY23, ₹18 in FY24, ₹66 in FY25, ₹46 in FY26) cause most of the jumps. Regular payouts alone (interim plus final) grew steadily: ₹43, ₹48, ₹55, ₹60 and ₹64.

FY = April–March. Amounts are totals declared for each year, built from interim, special and final dividends in Business Today and exchange-based dividend tables. One portal lists FY26 as ₹109; the components add to ₹110. An interim dividend of ₹12 for FY27 has an ex-date of 14 Oct 2026.

Company Stock Analysis: Tata Consultancy Services (TCS)

Task 2 · Listed on NSE India (TCS) and BSE (532540) · Data as at 9 October 2026

1. Introduction and Company Overview

Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) is an Indian IT services and consulting company and part of the Tata Group. It offers IT services, consulting and business solutions to large companies around the world. Tata Sons owned about 71.7% of its shares at 31 March 2026. TCS listed in 2004. This report studies its share price, valuation and dividends using public data.

2. Share Price Trend

The chart uses dated prices from public sources (1 January to 9 October 2026). A full verified monthly series for 12 months was not available, so the lines join only the dated points. The price moved from about ₹3,225 on 1 January to ₹2,156 on 9 October, a fall of about 33.1% over this period. Portals report a one-year fall of roughly 29–31%. The 52-week range is ₹1,976.80 to ₹3,350.00. The reasons are not verified; commonly discussed ones are careful global technology spending and worry about AI reducing demand for routine IT work.

4. Dividend History (FY22 – FY26)

5. Future Growth and Risks

  • Opportunities: AI services and automation, cloud migration, digital transformation of legacy systems, and cybersecurity.
  • Risks: slower client spending in the US and Europe; AI tools reducing routine work; strong competition and price pressure; currency, wage, attrition and visa-policy effects.

6. Conclusion

TCS has a strong market position, a long dividend record and a lower valuation than earlier in 2026. However, its share price has fallen by about 30% in a year and IT demand is uncertain. The picture is mixed. This report only summarises public data and does not recommend any action.
